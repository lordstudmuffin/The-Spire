# Creature and character models

Generated by scripts/_creatures_md.gd on Godot 4.3. Sizes are rest-pose bounding boxes in metres at scale 1 (x width, y height, z length) and are rough for skinned FBX models (bone extents inflate them). Quaternius FBX models are authored ~3-4x life size, so AssetLib.creature() multiplies their scale by AssetLib.FBX_SCALE (0.3): at scl = 1.0 a Wolf is ~1 m tall, a Dragon ~4 m. See anime-rpg-shots/catalog_creatures.png (1 m reference posts next to each model). Load with `AssetLib.creature(parent, pack, name, pos)` which returns [instance, AnimationPlayer]; play clips with `AssetLib.play_clip(ap, "idle")`.
